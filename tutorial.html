Step 1 of 8 · Document Archetype

Step 1: Name meaning and its meaningful parts

We begin with one release plan. In Document Archetype, the content flows as an inline article. The release-task kind declares explanation, demonstration, controls, evidence, and conclusion. Navigator, stage, and inspector are stable semantic places. Their encounter can change without renaming the parts or replacing their nodes.

Active Archetype: Document (reading)
Layout: default (2D flat plane)
Lattice: continuous (linear traversal)
Try this: Click a task in the Navigator or below to see its details load into the Inspector.
Step 2 of 8 · IDE Workspace Archetype

Step 2: Presentation changes; work survives

Notice what just happened: the interface morphed into a multi-pane IDE Workspace (left project tree, center editor, right inspector, bottom terminal). Crucially: your selected task is still selected, and your draft notes are preserved.

Active Archetype: Workspace (IDE)
Layout: 3col (multi-column 2D plane)
Lattice: app (internal frame viewports)
Try this: Type some private notes in the Inspector at right. Notice the tab in the editor reflects the unsaved draft!
Step 3 of 8 · Layer Projections

Step 3: Layer elevates planes into a 3D stack

When auxiliary panels (the Navigator and Inspector) shouldn't compete for 2D space on the flat plane, Atmosphere's Layer axis (Level Across Yield Evaluates Relationships) elevates them into the top layer as on-demand slide-out drawers.

Active Dimension: Layer (3D level & projection)
High-level rule: "When on mobile, area panel is a left slide-out; details is right slide-out."
Step 4 of 8 · Slide Deck Archetype

Step 4: The Slide Deck Archetype

Now the entire workspace becomes a Presentation Slide Deck! The central stage sequences horizontally, and the Navigator drops down as a tab from the top (top-menu layer projection).

Active Archetype: Presentation (Slides)
Lattice: slides (lateral traversal across tied totals)
Layer: top-menu (elevated dropdown plane)
Try this: Use the Previous/Next buttons on the slide stage or arrow keys to advance slides.

Step 6 of 8 · Dashboard Archetype

Step 6: High-level KPI and monitoring dashboards
